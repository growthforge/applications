How the proposed order is worked out

Addressable hours a month is how many times a month the task happens multiplied by the minutes it takes today. The proposed order divides that by effort (Low counts one, Medium two, High four). A request with a field missing is not ranked; it goes back to the person who raised it. The Lead sets the final order and can override any of it.
