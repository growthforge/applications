AI workflow assessment, for Cint

The reconciliation cycle, redrawn

One Cint workflow, assessed from public pages as I would in my first weeks. I chose the reconciliation cycle because it is structured, measured and crosses teams; I would repeat the method with each function in the first 90 days.

The map shows how those pages describe the work. Every sticking point is a hypothesis to test in week 1, with the people who do the work.

  • Seven ideas: two proceed, one reshaped, two parked, two stopped.
  • Ten steps: AI makes no decision of record; two that appear to work today are left alone.
  • A saving counts only while the appeal-overturn rate holds.

Before the 25th

  1. 1Remind buyers before the deadline Automated Template only None Reconciled on time
    Hypothesis
    Some eligible target groups reach the deadline without anyone having looked.
    Week-1 test
    Count last month's target groups still open in the final week before the deadline.
    If wrong
    If almost none are late, drop the step.
    Today
    PMs see target groups eligible this month and next in the "eligible to submit" tab; the deadline is the 25th of the month after each RID's timestamp.
    Ceiling in full
    Rules send a reminder from an approved template to the PM and the CSM; no model; cannot change eligibility, dates or amounts.
    Owner and partners
    CSMs and account managers. Business Technology builds it on the Slack and Salesforce layer Cint has described.
  2. 2Keep each cycle rule stated once Safely autonomous Opens tickets; edits nothing; logged InfoSec Open conflicts
    Hypothesis
    The rules sit on more pages than anyone owns, so an assistant answering from them would inherit any difference.
    Week-1 test
    List every page that states the deadline, the codes or the thresholds, and compare them.
    If wrong
    If they agree and one owner holds them, a quarterly review is enough; drop the check.
    Today
    The deadline, the reason codes and the programme thresholds are stated across policy, help-centre and developer pages.
    Ceiling in full
    A nightly check reads published pages and opens a ticket for the content owner when two disagree; it may not edit or publish anything; every run is logged; the owner can switch it off.
    Owner and partners
    Support and documentation owners. InfoSec reviews the check once; Business Technology builds it. Links to the second idea in the cut.
  3. Buyers correct rejected files (out of scope) Product's Load passed to Product
    Today
    A failed file cannot be corrected mid-upload; the buyer downloads the error report, fixes the original and starts again.
    Decision
    Validation is a product feature that already checks formats and reasons. Not the Office's to redesign; the Office passes the support load this creates to Product as evidence.
    Owner
    The buyer, in the product.

Audit window

  1. 3Screen flagged reversals AI-assistedEscalates Drafts a pack; decides nothing DPO, InfoSec Minutes per screen; appeal overturns
    Hypothesis
    Gathering the evidence takes longer than judging it.
    Week-1 test
    Time three screens with an analyst, split into gathering and judging.
    If wrong
    If most of the time is judgement, reshape the step: a checklist rather than a pack.
    Today
    Cint validates every reversal submitted with a reason, and specialists run manual investigations for part of the work. Acceptable reasons include Straight-lining (932) and Speeder (933); survey-side errors such as wrong qualifications or quotas (942) are not acceptable.
    Ceiling in full
    Drafts an evidence pack and a reviewer note; no decision, no message, no status change. The gates section shows the output, and the build pack runs it.
    Owner and partners
    Trust and Safety Operations. DPO and InfoSec gate it; AI/Data Architecture and Business Technology build it; the Enablement Lead trains analysts on it.
    Escalates to
    The Trust and Safety lead, when a pattern suggests a coordinated fraud source.
  2. 4Send the audit notice and form AutomatedLikely exists today Leave as is None Forms back in time
    Hypothesis
    This appears to work; the wait on the buyer's form is the right wait.
    Week-1 test
    Confirm how the notice goes out on the Exchange, and how many forms came back after their deadline last quarter.
    If wrong
    If the notice is sent by hand, automate it; if late forms are common, add one reminder; otherwise leave as is.
    Today
    When a reconciliation is audited, the PM is notified with a time frame to respond, and Cint's audit and final approval may run to the last calendar day of the month. The Marketplace help page describes the email as auto-generated, with the survey number, the RIDs and a form with a deadline.
    Owner
    Trust and Safety Operations.
  3. 5Overturn or uphold the reversal Human-ledEscalates Side-by-side view only DPO; decision of record Appeal overturns; time to proficiency
    Hypothesis
    The decision waits on finding the supplier's context, not on weighing it.
    Week-1 test
    Sit with two analysts on live decisions and note where the waiting happens.
    If wrong
    If the context is already to hand, drop the side-by-side view.
    Today
    Reasons are reviewed, and late or invalid reversals are overturned.
    Ceiling in full
    AI places the buyer's form and the supplier context side by side and makes no recommendation; the analyst decides and records the reason.
    Kept human on purpose
    For analysts in their first months, the side-by-side view opens only after they have recorded their own view, so they still build the judgement.
    Owner and partners
    Trust and Safety Operations. The DPO screens the use of buyer forms; CSMs and account managers take escalations; the Enablement Lead supports new analysts.
    Escalates to
    The account lead, for disputed high-value cases.

First business day

  1. 6Compute rates against thresholds AutomatedLikely exists today Rules, no model None Negative Reconciliation Rate
    Hypothesis
    This appears to be automated already: rules and a published formula, with no judgement in it.
    Week-1 test
    Ask the reviewers whether anything is recalculated by hand before the first business day.
    If wrong
    Fix the data feed, still with no model. A model would add risk and no value here.
    Today
    RISP reviews hard-close rates and POP compares per-country soft-close rates with a 5% standard, using a published formula, on the first business day of each month. Suppliers already see the same rates in their reports and dashboards.
    Owners
    Trust and Safety Operations and the supply team.
  2. 7Assign statuses, write the root cause AI-assisted Drafts; thresholds set the status Legal template Minutes per status
    Hypothesis
    The first business day is a peak because every review lands at once, and the root-cause write-up is its slowest part.
    Week-1 test
    Ask reviewers to log minutes per status for one review day.
    If wrong
    If the write-up is quick, the draft is not worth a Legal template; drop it.
    Today
    Statuses follow published thresholds: for POP, Watchlist from 5% to under 10%, Needs Attention from 10% to under 40%, Alarming at 40% or more. RISP notices go out in a fixed sequence with the CSM and account manager copied, and buyers are expected to run a root-cause analysis and an action plan with their CSM.
    Ceiling in full
    Drafts the root-cause summary by supplier, country and source from that month's packs; the status comes from the published thresholds, never from the model; a person approves every notice before it is sent.
    Owners and partners
    Trust and Safety Operations (RISP) and the supply team (POP). Legal approves the template once; CSMs and account managers; the Enablement Lead.

After the review

  1. 8Review supplier plans of action AI-assisted Flags gaps; drafts only DPO Days to agreed plan
    Hypothesis
    Plans are checked against the supplier's reversals by hand, and gaps are found late.
    Week-1 test
    Review five recent plans with the supply team: how long each took to check, and how many came back.
    If wrong
    If plans are checked quickly and rarely come back, drop the step.
    Today
    Suppliers in Needs Attention fill out a plan of action and review their security and quality audit form; the supply team gathers context from the buy-side team, and buyer error leads to a Retrospective Exclusion.
    Ceiling in full
    Compares each plan with the drivers in that supplier's reversals, flags gaps and assembles the buyer-error evidence; drafts only; reuses the step 3 integration and log.
    Owner and partners
    The supply and buy-side teams. The DPO; the Enablement Lead.
  2. 9Pause, suspend or exclude Human-ledEscalates No AI recommendation Decision of record Decisions inside published windows
    Hypothesis
    Delays come from gathering approvals, not from the rules.
    Week-1 test
    Trace three recent pauses from status to decision against the published windows.
    If wrong
    If decisions already land inside the windows, leave the step alone.
    Today
    POP pauses a supplier's traffic after 10 business days in Needs Attention or 2 in Alarming, unless the supplier acts within that window. RISP gives buyers two calendar months to improve before a possible suspension.
    Ceiling in full
    A person makes every decision that affects a supplier's revenue or a buyer's rights; AI gives no recommendation of record.
    Owners
    The supply team and Trust and Safety Operations.
    Escalates to
    At Multiple Strikes L2, the contract is reviewed with the supply team.
  3. 10Answer adjustment and payout queries AI-assisted Drafts replies; posts nothing Legal template, InfoSec Days to answer; reopened queries
    Hypothesis
    Most of a query's handling time goes on tracing adjustment lines back to RIDs.
    Week-1 test
    Time three recent queries with Finance.
    If wrong
    If the time goes on the conversation rather than the tracing, reshape to an approved reply template only.
    Today
    Buyers are billed monthly on total completes, with reconciliations credited on the next month's invoice. Reconciliation adjustments are not returned through the supplier API. The total of reconciliation-eligible costs is shown in USD only.
    Ceiling in full
    Traces the adjustment lines on a query back to the RIDs and drafts the reply; Finance approves material adjustments and every reply; posting stays system-driven; no posting, no credit and no reply sent without approval.
    Owner and partners
    Finance. Legal approves the reply template; InfoSec reviews the integration; AI/Data Architecture and Business Technology build it; CSMs and account managers; the Enablement Lead.

What the evidence pack returns (step 3)

Build and control
Trigger
A reversal flagged for screening in the existing queue.
Reads
The reason code and RIDs, a completion-time summary, the target group's set-up history, the supplier's recent rates. Open-ended answers only if the DPO screen allows; minimised otherwise.
Writes
An evidence pack and a draft reviewer note. Nothing else.
Output contract
Every claim carries its source; confidence is stated; "not enough evidence" is a valid answer; the code check uses the published list, not the model's memory.
Gate
The analyst decides. Nothing reaches a buyer without an explicit approval.
Log
Every pack, every edit and every decision, so edits become feedback.
Stop rule
Switch off and fall back to today's manual route if the appeal-overturn rate rises above its baseline in any cycle.
Ceiling
AI-assisted. Any change goes to the Steering Group with evidence.
Reuse
Steps 7 and 8 use the same integration and log.
Route
The build, buy or configure choice is made with AI/Data Architecture, and Business Technology delivers and supports it on the Slack and Salesforce layer Cint has described, where Enterprise Search and an AI Slackbot are already live.
Tooling
For the prototype I would use whatever the architecture team approves. I have built workflows in n8n for over a year and build with Claude, Gemini and GPT, so the tool is the smaller decision.

Deployment, adoption and outcome, kept apart

Baseline first: self-reported savings are weak evidence.

Deployment
Screens with a complete, sourced pack.
Adoption
Packs opened; drafts accepted with light edits.
Outcome
Days from flag to decision; analyst hours per cycle.
Quality guard
Appeal-overturn rate at or below baseline.
People
Time to proficiency for new analysts.

A saving counts only while the guard holds, and released hours count only once someone names where they go.
