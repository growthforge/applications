5+ years in finance and operations; Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900); MSc Finance and Banking. What I have delivered

Independent work for a job application. Not produced, reviewed or endorsed by Vodafone. Built from public information only.

If I joined: the first 90 days

  1. Days 1 to 30 Learn the AI learning strategy and impact framework1, map what exists, agree the reporting data. Exit: the account-manager brief, signed.
  2. Days 31 to 60 Prototype stage one with five account managers and two team leaders; set the baseline. Exit: a tested stage one.
  3. Days 61 to 90 First cohort live in one market. Exit: its stage one report and the next brief. A second market waits for the week-12 report.

This builds on what Vodafone has already built rather than starting fresh: Copilot Skill Labs, its champions and agents such as the RFP Assistant, recognised with a 2025 DWG award for AI Enablement at Scale.2

Would you send this?

Illustrative

The practice check from module M3, its fifth screen: three drafts, about three minutes. The customer is fictional.

Larchmoor Freight (fictional): meeting notes, Tuesday

  • Renew the mobile plan for 60 drivers before the contract ends in March.
  • Asked about tracking for 25 trailers. No pricing discussed.
  • Asked whether tracking data is stored in the UK. You said you would check.
  • Next step: a summary, and a call with a solutions specialist, who is free Thursday at 10:00 or Friday at 14:00.

Copilot drafted each message from these notes. What would you do with each draft?

Draft 1 of 3

Thank you for Tuesday. You would like to renew the mobile plan for your 60 drivers before March, and to know more about tracking for your 25 trailers, which we will include at no extra cost. I will confirm where tracking data is stored. Could we speak with our solutions specialist on Thursday at 10:00 or Friday at 14:00?

Show the expected answer and why

Expected: Fix it, then send. Nobody offered tracking at no extra cost, and nobody asked for a price. Your notes can put this right on their own: delete the clause and send. The check: underline every commitment and find it in the source.

If you chose Send it: ‘At no extra cost’ is a commitment nobody made.

If you chose Stop and escalate: Close, but this one is yours to fix. Nobody asked for a price, so nobody needs to decide one: delete the offer and send.

Draft 2 of 3

Thank you for Tuesday. You would like to renew the mobile plan for your 60 drivers before March, and to know more about tracking for your 25 trailers. I will confirm where tracking data is stored. Could we speak with our solutions specialist on Thursday at 10:00 or Friday at 14:00?

Show the expected answer and why

Expected: Send it. Every statement is in the notes, including the specialist’s two times, and the draft decides nothing that is not yours. The data question stays an action: route it to the right specialist today, whatever happens to the draft. Not every AI draft needs changing; the skill is knowing which ones do.

If you chose Fix it, then send: There is nothing to fix: each sentence traces to the notes. Editing a good draft spends the time the tool saved.

If you chose Stop and escalate: The draft makes no decision for anyone else. Route the data question to its specialist, and send this summary.

Draft 3 of 3

On Wednesday the customer replies: “Can the new plan start in January, at our current price? We need to know by Friday.” Copilot drafts your answer:

Yes, we can start your new plan in January at your current price. I will send the paperwork today.

Show the expected answer and why

Expected: Stop and escalate. The customer is asking for a decision on price and contract dates, and the draft makes it for you. Nothing in your notes can put that right; only whoever approves pricing and terms can. Do not send. Take the request to them today, and tell the customer when to expect an answer. The rule: fix what your notes can put right; escalate what only someone else can decide.

If you chose Send it: It agrees a price and a start date that nobody with the authority has agreed.

If you chose Fix it, then send: Rewording cannot supply the decision. A reply saying you will check is the holding line that goes with escalating; the request itself still needs its owner.

You matched the expected answer on 0 of 3 items.

In the stage checks, items in this format score a decision, not a feeling, and the share of expected decisions feeds the quality measure below. The full module check adds an optional “How sure are you?” after each answer; it is used to review items and never reported as capability, because confidence can rise without learning.

Module M3, screen by screen: Notes to follow-up in 15 minutes

Every module in the pathway follows this shape.

  1. 1

    Objective

    “You will turn your own meeting notes into a follow-up that promises only what the notes support.”

    1 min
  2. 2

    Hook from a real task

    A follow-up that promised a delivery date nobody agreed reaches the customer’s finance director. “What went wrong?”

    1 min
  3. 3

    Worked example

    A captioned recording: the prompt built in four parts from the Larchmoor notes (goal, context, source, expectations).

    3 min
  4. 4

    Good and flawed output

    The same prompt, two drafts. “Underline every commitment and find it in the notes.”

    2 min
  5. 5

    Practice

    The three “Would you send this?” drafts.

    3 min
  6. 6

    Feedback

    Each choice gets its own feedback, naming the rule: fix what your notes can put right; escalate what only someone else can decide.

    1 min
  7. 7

    Check

    One new scored draft. The same rule returns in the stage two observed task.

    2 min
  8. 8

    Job aid handover

    Save the card to the team library and use it on your next real follow-up this week.

    2 min

Every screen: captions and a transcript for any recording, no timer, usable by keyboard alone, plain sentences. Designed to WCAG 2.2 AA.3

M3 Notes to follow-up

Illustrative
For
Account managers, after any customer meeting
Owner
Skill Lead (to be agreed)
Version
0.4, review by 1 February 2027

Task. Turn your meeting notes into a follow-up the customer can act on.

Prompt

  • Worked example
  • Guided
  • On your own

Worked example

Goal: Draft a follow-up email to the Operations Director at Larchmoor Freight after today’s meeting.

Context: I am their account manager. They want to renew their mobile plan and asked about trailer tracking.

Source: use only my notes, ‘Larchmoor Freight notes, Tuesday’.

Expectations: under 150 words, plain British English, three bullet points, one next step offering the specialist’s two times from the notes, and a list of anything you were unsure about.

Guided

Goal: Draft a [type of message] to [who] about [what].

Context: I am [your role]; they want [their aim].

Source: use only [the file or thread].

Expectations: [length], [tone], [structure], and a list of anything you were unsure about.

The four parts follow Microsoft’s prompt guidance. Only the goal is required, but the source line is what stops the draft inventing.4

On your own

Write the prompt yourself. It passes if it names the goal, names the only source Copilot may use, says what a good answer looks like, and asks Copilot to list what it was unsure about.

Market notes

Illustrative markets; the academy’s own languages are not public. Language support as Microsoft lists it.5

  • UK
  • Germany
  • Kenya

UK

Currency GBP. Dates as 3 March 2027. Ask for plain British English in the expectations.

Germany

Currency EUR. Dates as 03.03.2027. Formal address (Sie) for customer emails. Keep the notes in German and ask for German output, so the source and the draft match. German is on Microsoft’s supported-language list.

Kenya

Currency KES. Dates as 3 March 2027. Microsoft’s language list shows Swahili for voice on personal accounts only, not for text on work accounts, so the card and its checks stay in English, and a fluent colleague checks any customer message in Swahili before it is sent.

Check before you send

  • Every commitment appears in the notes or the contract.
  • Names, dates and figures match the source.
  • Nothing from another customer appears.
  • The tone is one you would sign.

Stop and escalate when

Putting the draft right needs a decision you do not own: price, contract terms, where data is held, or security. Do not send. Ask the owner, and tell the customer when to expect an answer.

A question you said you would check is yours to route: send it to its owner today, whatever happens to the draft.

Data rule

In line with Vodafone’s published approach of limiting public AI tools, customer contracts and pricing are used only in approved company tools.6

More on this card

A good and a flawed draft

Good: “...you would like to know more about tracking for your 25 trailers. I will confirm where tracking data is stored.”

Flawed: “...tracking for your 25 trailers, which we will include at no extra cost.” Commits to an offer nobody made.

Tool notes

Microsoft 365 Copilot in Outlook or Word: name the notes file in the Source line so the source is explicit. Other approved assistants: the same four parts apply; only the way you attach the source changes.

Why each field is there

  • Task and role, because learning starts from the job.
  • Four-part prompt, because it matches Microsoft’s own prompt guidance.
  • Good and flawed draft, because a non-example teaches the check.
  • Check, because verification belongs at the point of use.
  • Escalate, because the learner needs a named route for decisions they do not own.
  • Data rule, because it carries Vodafone’s published guardrails.
  • Owner, version and review date, because feature names and guidance change, so every card expires.

How would you know it worked?

The VLO business impact framework is not public, so I would report in four terms and align them with it in my first weeks. Three answer the measures this role tracks: capability growth, AI adoption and productivity impact. Experience adds the learner’s view.

Term, and the question it answersEvidence I would useWhat it cannot show
Experience Learner reaction: did it help with the job?“Useful for my task?” after each module, and “used it at work this week?” in the weekly practice slotWhether anything was learned
Quality Capability growth: can they make the call?Module checks, stage checks and the observed task, scoredWhether they do it at work, unprompted
Learning transfer AI adoption: is it a weekly habit?Novice to Habitual or Power users over twelve weeks in the Copilot Dashboard, against a comparison groupWhether the use is good use
Business impact Productivity: did the work get better?Time to a checked follow-up, and follow-up quality in a blind review, against a baseline and a comparison groupCause, without a designed comparison

Never reported as impact: completions, logins, licences, satisfaction on its own, or self-reported minutes added together.

Mind the baseline window. Microsoft reports email draft and summary metrics were underestimated from 1 June 2025 to 18 February 2026. A baseline there understates the start; a comparison across it overstates the gain.7

Choosing the next role and market

  • Frequent tasks that can be checked against a source.
  • A real data or commitment risk the check can teach.
  • A Skill Lead and a Partner ready to own it.
  • Reporting is possible: job-function data loaded, and groups large enough to show, because the Copilot Dashboard hides small groups.
  • The tool supports the market’s language for the task.
Questions I would ask before drafting a brief
  • Which three writing tasks take the most time each week?
  • Which mistake would cost most with a customer?
  • What data does the role touch, and where may it go?
  • Who signs off a commitment, a price or a data answer?
  • What already exists for this role: library content, Skill Labs, Copilot Academy?
  • Which team leaders will give the practice slot fifteen minutes a week?

Enablement I have delivered

I build AI tools, train the people who use them, and write work up so others can reuse it.

  • MTest Labs. I built a test-design tool on the firm’s own test-case library and issue logs, added an assistant the team could ask about the firm’s knowledge, and trained the team to run it. The testers stay the subject-matter experts: the tool drafts each case and a tester approves it before it reaches a client. Test design fell from an 8-hour day, or 2 to 3 days, to 1 to 3 supervised hours.
  • Bnk To The Future. Templates I created were adopted across the firm. With redesigned case routing, triage and escalation, turnaround fell by ~25% across 100+ cases.
  • A university enterprise network. I captured sessions as structured, reusable documentation with worked use cases, so others left with practical material.
  • AirTM. In a remote team matching daily payment volumes across currencies, I wrote root-cause fixes up as standard procedures, held a 95%+ service level, and cleared a 150-case backlog in three weeks.

Across countries: 5+ years in finance and operations, spanning client work for an accountancy practice in India, VAT return support for UK clients and the remote international team at AirTM. I use Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio, Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, and hold Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900).
