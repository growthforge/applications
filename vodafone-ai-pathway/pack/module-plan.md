# Module plan: AI pathway for account managers

> **Illustrative.** A sample plan written for an independent job application. Not Vodafone material; not produced, reviewed or endorsed by Vodafone. Every owner, SME and source is a proposal to be agreed with the Skill Lead and the Learning and Performance Business Partner. It assumes nothing about internal formats, data or teams.

Companion page: https://growthforge.github.io/applications/vodafone-ai-pathway/ (section “The pathway”).

## The rule for every module

Curate first, buy second, build only what Vodafone alone can write: its data rules, its checks and its examples. Every curated or bought item passes the content quality check (`partner-quality-check.md`) before it goes live.

Every digital module follows the M3 shape (`m3-storyboard.md`): objective, hook from a real task, worked example, good and flawed output, practice, feedback, one scored check item, job aid handover. Each ends with the experience questions “Was this useful for a task you do?” and “Will you use it at work this week?”; the weekly practice slot asks “Did you use it at work this week?”

## Stages

| Stage | Weeks | Time | You can | Check that closes the stage | LTEM tier, in plain words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. First useful result | 1 to 2 | About 90 minutes | Brief yourself for a customer meeting with Copilot, and check every name, date and figure against its source | Six decision items | Tier 5, decision-making: can you make the right call in a realistic case? |
| 2. A weekly workflow | 3 to 9 | About four hours | Tell a draft you can send from one you must fix or escalate | One observed task on supplied, realistic notes, scored on the four-part rubric | Tier 6, task performance: can you do the task well, in a realistic setting? |
| 3. Own and teach | 10 to 12 | About an hour | Improve a team prompt card, test it on three real cases and teach it to a colleague | A work sample from real work, confirmed by the manager, and a contributed card | Tier 7, transfer to work: do you do it in your real work? |

**Totals:** stage one 90 minutes, stage two 240, stage three 65; 395 minutes, about six and a half hours in all, across twelve weeks. Of the seven modules, M1 and M4 are curated, M5 is bought, and M2, M3, M6 and M7 are built.

## Stage two rubric (summary)

Four criteria, each scored 0, 1 or 2: accurate to the source; commits only to what is supported; the data rule kept; fit for the customer (tone, clarity, a clear next step). The standard is 6 of 8 with no zero on the data rule. A zero on the data rule triggers a review of M2 for the whole cohort. Full descriptors: `skills-check-item-bank.json`, `rubrics.stage_two_observed_task`.

## Module checks and how they are read

Each digital module’s screen 7 holds one scored decision item. After each cohort, report per module: the share choosing the expected option (item facility) and the share who used the module at work that week. A module whose check falls below 0.4 (fewer than 4 in 10 learners choose the expected answer), or which the fewest learners used, is revised before the next cohort. M6 is a team-meeting module with no digital check: it is read from the cards contributed and the stage three work sample. Full rules: `skills-check-item-bank.json`, `scoring`.
