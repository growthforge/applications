# Pathway brief: account managers

> **Illustrative.** A filled sample brief written for an independent job application. Not Vodafone material; not produced, reviewed or endorsed by Vodafone. Every filled value is a proposal to be tested with the Skill Lead and the Learning and Performance Business Partner. Built from public information only; it assumes nothing about internal formats, data or teams.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Account managers selling to business customers |
| Pilot market | To be agreed |
| Brief version and date | 0.4, 3 November 2026 |
| Pathway length | Twelve weeks, because Microsoft’s Copilot Dashboard counts someone as a Habitual or Power user only after use in at least 9 of the past 12 weeks, so the transfer measure can be read with data that already exists |

## The seven fields

| Field | This pathway |
|---|---|
| Role and real tasks | Meeting brief, follow-up, proposal section, account plan |
| Data rule | Contracts and pricing only in approved company tools (in line with Vodafone’s published approach of limiting public AI tools) |
| The check | Every commitment traced to the notes or the contract |
| Escalate | Any reply that needs a decision on price, contract terms, where data is held or security. Fix what your notes can put right; escalate what only someone else can decide. Open questions are routed to their owner the same day. |
| Team leaders | Briefed before week 1; model it in week 1; set the standard for “checked” in week 3; protect the 15-minute practice slot, weeks 3 to 9; confirm each work sample in week 12 |
| Who owns what | **Skill Lead:** tasks, standard, stage checks. **Learning and Performance Business Partner:** market, cohort, team-leader time, local examples. **Specialist:** design, curation, partners, quality check, report. **The teams that own Copilot reporting and HR data:** the job-function data |
| Signed | Skill Lead; Learning and Performance Business Partner; date (not yet signed) |

## Stages

| Stage | Weeks | Time | You can | Check that closes the stage (LTEM tier, in plain words) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. First useful result | 1 to 2 | About 90 minutes | Brief yourself for a customer meeting with Copilot, and check every name, date and figure against its source | Six decision items (Tier 5, decision-making: the right call in a realistic case) |
| 2. A weekly workflow | 3 to 9 | About four hours | Tell a draft you can send from one you must fix or escalate | One observed task on supplied, realistic notes, scored on the four-part rubric (Tier 6, task performance: the task done well in a realistic setting) |
| 3. Own and teach | 10 to 12 | About an hour | Improve a team prompt card, test it on three real cases and teach it to a colleague | A work sample from real work that your manager confirms (Tier 7, transfer to work) |

Totals: stage one 90 minutes, stage two 240, stage three 65; 395 minutes, about six and a half hours in all. Of the seven modules, M1 and M4 are curated, M5 is bought, and M2, M3, M6 and M7 are built.

## Measures

| Term | Evidence | What it cannot show |
|---|---|---|
| Experience (learner reaction: did it help with the job?) | “Useful for my task?” after each module, and “used it at work this week?” in the weekly practice slot | Whether anything was learned |
| Quality (capability growth: can they make the call?) | Module checks, stage checks and the observed task, scored | Whether they do it at work, unprompted |
| Learning transfer (AI adoption: is it a weekly habit?) | Novice to Habitual or Power users over twelve weeks, from the Copilot Dashboard, against a comparison group | Whether the use is good use |
| Business impact (productivity: did the work get better?) | Time to a checked follow-up, and follow-up quality in a blind review, against a pre-launch baseline and a comparison group | Cause, without a designed comparison |

Offered as a default to align with the VLO business impact framework in the first weeks. Definitions: `metric-dictionary.csv`. Baseline note: Microsoft reports that Copilot email draft and summary metrics were underestimated from 1 June 2025 to 18 February 2026; a baseline from that window understates the start, and a comparison across it overstates the gain.

## Next briefs to test against the five rules

| Role | Top tasks | Data rule | The check | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer care team leaders | Coaching notes from call reviews; shift handover summaries | Customer account details only in approved tools | Every coaching point traced to the call record | Microsoft 365 Copilot |
| Network operations | Incident updates for stakeholders; change write-ups | No network configuration or site details outside approved tools | Every time and impact figure traced to the incident log | Claude or ChatGPT Enterprise, where approved for the team |
| HR business partners | Policy answers; job descriptions | No employee personal data in a prompt unless the tool is approved for it | Every policy answer cites its clause | Microsoft 365 Copilot |
| Finance business partners | Variance commentary; board-pack summaries | Unpublished results only in approved tools | Every figure ties to the ledger | Copilot in Excel |

Illustrative: tasks and rules to be confirmed with each Skill Lead. The four-part prompt and the check stay the same whichever approved tool a role uses.
