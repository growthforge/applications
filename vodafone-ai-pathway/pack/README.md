# Take-away pack: From first prompt to weekly habit

> **Illustrative.** Working files behind the page at https://growthforge.github.io/applications/vodafone-ai-pathway/ , written by Hemanth Vallamkonda for an independent job application. Not Vodafone material; not produced, reviewed or endorsed by Vodafone. Customers, names and figures in the examples are fictional.

| File | What it is | Who would use it |
|---|---|---|
| `prompt-card-m3.pdf` | The M3 prompt card on one printable A4 page | Account managers, at the moment of work |
| `prompt-card-m3.md` | The same card as editable Markdown, plus the card review checklist | Whoever owns the team card library |
| `skills-check-item-bank.json` | Ten decision items (three practice, seven scored) with the expected answer, rationale and per-option feedback, the shared answer sets, the rules, the scoring and item-review rules, and the stage two and stage three rubrics | E-learning developers and the Skill Lead |

| `kickoff-run-sheet.md` | The week 1 live kick-off, minute by minute: what happens, facilitator moves and script lines, an online variant, the follow-up task, and the team leader’s “Model it” briefing for the first team meeting | The Specialist, Copilot champions and team leaders |
| `pathway-brief-template.md` | The one-page brief every pathway starts from in this design, with guidance per field, the build, curate or buy table, the measures and the content quality check | The Specialist, with a Skill Lead and a Learning and Performance Business Partner |
| `pathway-brief-account-managers.md` | The template filled for account managers: tasks, data rule, owners, modules with sources, measures, first 90 days, the next four roles, open items | The two people who would sign it |
| `partner-quality-check.md` | The seven-question content quality check, a partner register of terms to agree (no scores for any partner), example service levels by severity, the quarterly review agenda and how a review feeds renewal | Whoever manages content partners |
| `metric-dictionary.csv` | Fifteen measures: definition, source system class, level, cadence, owner and the anti-vanity rule for each | Whoever reports impact |

## How the files fit together

1. The **brief** fixes the role, its real tasks, the data rule, the check and who owns what. The **module plan** sets out every module and where its content comes from.
2. The **run sheet** opens the pathway live in week 1 and hands over to the team leaders, who model it in their first team meeting.
3. The **storyboard** turns one task into a 15-minute module; its practice and check screens draw their items from the **item bank**.
4. The module ends by handing over the **prompt card**, which carries the same check, escalate rule and data rule into real work. The rule throughout: fix what your notes can put right; escalate what only someone else can decide.
5. Curated and bought content passes the **partner quality check** before it goes live and is reviewed each quarter.
6. The **metric dictionary** defines how the pathway is reported, in four terms, with what each measure cannot show.
